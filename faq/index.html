FAQ

What is Unstable Innovation about?

It is a book about innovation, entrepreneurship, and philosophy. It teaches how to embrace chaos, uncertainty, and acceleration as the path to real innovation. Nelson draws on Stoicism, game theory, megatrends, and his career across aviation, finance, education, and Bitcoin.

Who is this book for?

Innovators, entrepreneurs, students, and leaders who feel that corporate frameworks are not enough. It is also for anyone navigating the gap between what they were taught and what actually works in fast-changing environments.

Is the book really free? Why?

Yes. Nelson wants the ideas to reach as many people as possible, so the PDF, EPUB, and Kindle versions are free directly from this site. In return he asks for two things: follow him on LinkedIn, and subscribe to Spark News (max one email per month). Bitcoin donations via Geyser Fund are welcome, never required.

PDF, EPUB, and Kindle (AZW3), all free, all instant. Head to the free download page. If you prefer the physical book, it is available on Amazon as a paperback in 13 Amazon storefronts worldwide.

Who is Nelson Inno?

Nelson Inno is a Salvadoran entrepreneur, founder and CEO of WeSpark, TEDx speaker, and author of Unstable Innovation. He builds AI-powered education systems for large enterprises, governments, and universities, and co-designed Bitcoin Diploma 2.0 with El Salvador's National Bitcoin Office, a curriculum the Ministry of Education plans to bring to up to 500,000 students. His official personal website is nelsoninno.com.

Does Nelson work with enterprises or governments on AI, Bitcoin, or innovation projects?

Yes. Nelson works with innovation departments, digital-transformation teams, and technology teams inside large enterprises and governments on three areas: AI education, training, and implementation; Bitcoin education and implementation; and innovation and technology transformation. The fastest way to reach him is LinkedIn. It is the only channel he uses for engagement inquiries.

Is this book good for teenagers and young adults?

Yes. Unstable Innovation is a short, easy-to-read book that gives teenagers and young adults their first serious taste of philosophy, alongside concrete examples of ethics, resilience, and purpose. Parents give it to their kids as an early guide to why their actions matter, why bringing love and value to the world matters, and why effort is what builds a strong life.

What will a young reader take from this book?

A clear message that their actions matter, that they can bring both love and value to the world, and that they build strong lives through effort. The book pairs a short, readable format with philosophy that lasts: when life gets hard, they will already know it is supposed to be hard, and they will have the tools to push forward and become greater people, with strong ethical and moral principles as their compass.
