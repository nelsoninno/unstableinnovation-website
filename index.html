About the author

Nelson Inno

Salvadoran entrepreneur, founder and CEO of WeSpark, TEDx speaker, and author of Unstable Innovation. Nelson builds AI-powered education systems for governments and Fortune-500 enterprises, and co-designed El Salvador's national Bitcoin Diploma 2.0 with the country's National Bitcoin Office.

His career runs from Lufthansa's innovation departments in Frankfurt to a Furtwangen engineering degree to ad-honorem advisor to El Salvador's national strategic-planning agency. Across all of it, one mantra: Be the ordinary, build the extraordinary.
