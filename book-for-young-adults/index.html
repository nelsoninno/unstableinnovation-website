What you get

What will this book help me do?

It won't hand you a career plan. It helps you understand yourself and the world well enough to choose your next step, and then commit to it.

Read the world

Acceleration, not speed, drives change. The book maps the megatrends shaping the next decade, from AI to geopolitics, so you can see where opportunities are heading.

Spot your moment

Life gives uneven opportunities. The book shows how to recognize your moment of truth, and why hesitating can cost you the opportunity.

Stack useful skills

Storytelling, visual thinking, business modeling, video. Nelson shares the skills that kept paying off for him, and how he learned them.

Get unstuck

One of the book's favorite questions: “How can I make this problem ten thousand times worse?” Then do the opposite.

Commit to something

“Extraordinary accomplishments come from doing ordinary things for extraordinary periods of time.” Alex Hormozi, quoted in the book.

Define a good life

Eudaimonia, the Greek idea of flourishing, and the power of saying no to anything that doesn't move you toward it.

No pressure

Do I need to want to be an entrepreneur?

No. If you dream of starting a company, great. If you don't, that's also okay.

Unstable Innovation is about becoming someone who creates and adds value, whether you do it as an employee, a freelancer, a researcher or a founder. What matters is the mindset: curiosity, commitment and the courage to act when your moment shows up.

It will be hard. Perhaps it will be the hardest thing you ever do, but you will persevere.

Nelson Inno, Unstable Innovation

Questions young readers ask

Is Unstable Innovation a good book for people in their 20s?

Yes. It is written for people who feel the world changing fast and want a role in it. It helps young adults read the big trends, spot their moment of truth and commit to a problem worth solving.

Will it tell me what career to choose?

No. It won't hand you a plan. It helps you understand yourself and spot opportunities, so the choice is yours and better informed.

I don't want to start a company. Is it still for me?

Yes. The book is about becoming someone who creates and adds value, whether you do it as an employee, a freelancer, a researcher or a founder.

How long is Unstable Innovation?

215 pages in 9 chapters. Most people read it in about 3 to 4 hours.
